## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is the terrace at night: Cosenza lit below, the restaurant as the one warm sign on the hill. It refuses the category default of a full-bleed food photo with a serif name and a "Prenota" button.
OWN-WORLD: Night blue (#0e1828 → #1b2c46) owns the page; city-light amber (#f4c56a) is the only active colour, used for light, state and action; warm paper cream (#f5eee1) for text; garden green (#86a07a) only for "aperto" and vegetarian marks. Bricolage Grotesque (condensed, heavy) as lit-sign display; Atkinson Hyperlegible for reading. Components are signs and lit panels with no white cards: rules, dotted leaders, lit/unlit states. Raises: one shouting word APERTO/CHIUSO that never shrinks (TDR); a strict two-column menu grid and a small Arca mark hidden once per view (Art Paul); identical repeating menu units, name…price (VU bridge); total palette commitment with no neutral grey (one-bit).
STORY: The visitor sees that it is open tonight and that the view is the point, then reads the menu, then books in two taps. The owner recognises his own place.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A canvas night sky over layered hill silhouettes. The Cosenza valley floor is a field of amber light points that twinkle, with the restaurant's own lit sign on the near ridge. Top left: the "L'Arca di Noè" wordmark. Huge and centred: APERTO (or CHIUSO · riapre alle 19:00). Under it, the hours today and "Pizzeria · Ristorante · Griglieria, Trenta – Casali del Manco". Primary "Prenota un tavolo" in amber with a secondary "Chiama". A fixed thumb bar on mobile.

## Signature interaction
The lights are real-time aware: the sky hue follows the Europe/Rome clock (dusk before 19:00, deep night after), and the restaurant's sign on the ridge is lit only while it is actually open. Scroll lowers the camera: the hills parallax and the lights drift down into the next section.
