## Scope
Whole site (home, menu, prenota, eventi, dove siamo, privacy). Persuade surface executed as the category standard; menu and prenota are Operate.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The well-made restaurant website, executed at full craft. It is calm, light and readable, and it puts the practical information first. The user explicitly took the canon exit and rejected the "night sky" world as too AI-looking.
OWN-WORLD: White and warm-paper (#f5f2ec) surfaces, near-black ink, and one accent, Sila forest green (#2f4a3a). The footer and info bar are dark green-black. Headings are set in Libre Caslon Text, the text in Figtree. 4px radii, hairline rules, no shadows and no effects.
STORY: Visitors see the place and the food, find the hours, address and phone at once, read what the place offers (three kitchens, large rooms, garden, view, parking), then book or ask about an event.
FIRST VIEWPORT: A full-bleed wood-oven photo under a transparent header. The serif H1 reads "Pizzeria, ristorante e griglieria a Trenta, sopra Cosenza", with a subline and two buttons (Prenota, Menu). Below it sits a three-column strip: Orari (with live open/closed status), Indirizzo, Prenotazioni.
FORM: Canon (category standard); references are Berberè and Pepe in Grani structure; seed key 9a784d1c.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
