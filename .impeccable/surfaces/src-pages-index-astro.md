## Direction contract
THESIS: A Big Mamma–style Italian restaurant site adapted to a large Calabrian family venue. It is warm, editorial and impactful, while practical info stays one tap away.
OWN-WORLD: Cream paper surfaces, dark brown ink, and Calabrian red as the signature colour, which owns the events band and the buttons. Instrument Serif display headings carry italic accent words; small caps labels and body text are set in Figtree. Pill buttons, rounded and arched photo frames, collapsible info sections.
STORY: Visitors see the place and the food, find the hours, address and phone at once, read what the place offers (three kitchens, large rooms, garden, view, parking), then book or ask about an event.
